Since our first shutter opened on C.G. Road

Proudly Serving Ahmedabad

Drishti Eyewear began as a small optical counter in the heart of the city and has grown into a studio trusted by families across Ahmedabad for honest eye care and frames worth keeping. This is the story of how we got here — and the people who make it happen.

Our Story

A studio built on paying attention

Drishti means "sight" — but for us it always meant something a little wider than eyesight. It's the way a good pair of glasses lets you notice the small things again: a friend's face across a crowded thela, the fine print on a lease, the road ahead on a monsoon evening.

We started with one belief: that an eye test should feel like a conversation, not a transaction, and that a frame should be chosen the way you'd choose a good piece of furniture — built to last, and built for you.

Our beginning was a single 200 sq. ft. counter and a shared conviction that Ahmedabad deserved better eyewear.

Our Journey

Fifteen years, one city at a time

2023
Year One
The Beginning

A single counter store on C.G. Road, three trial frames, and a promise to never rush an eye test.

2024
Year Two
Growth

Word travelled through Navrangpura's old bazaars. We brought on our first optometrist and doubled our frame catalogue.

2025
Year Three
Expansion

Satellite and Vastrapur stores opened, each with an in-house lab so lenses could be ground the same day.

Now
Present Day
Serving Ahmedabad

Four studios, thousands of eye tests a year, and the same counter-side conversation we started with.
