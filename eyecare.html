Eye Care Tips

Small Habits, Better Vision

01

Screen Eye Care

Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

02

Healthy Eye Habits

Sleep, hydration and leafy greens matter more for eye health than most people expect.

03

Choosing the Right Lenses

Your lifestyle — desk job, driving, outdoors — should decide your lens, not just your prescription.
